# Globex SOP — Billing, proration & refunds

## Proration

Globex bills **annually, upgrades take effect immediately, and the delta is
invoiced on the next monthly true-up** — it is *not* prorated to the day.
This differs from the public/self-serve behaviour. If a customer disputes a
mid-cycle upgrade charge, explain the annual-plus-true-up model and offer to
send the true-up schedule.

## Refunds

Support **cannot** issue refunds. Route all refund requests to the Finance
queue with the invoice number. Standard outcome is account credit, not a
card refund, unless Finance approves otherwise.

## Discounts

Non-profit and volume discounts are handled by Sales. Support should not
quote or promise any discount — hand the request to the account's AE.
